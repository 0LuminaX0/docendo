## When something fails

Fix it yourself, in the spirit of CLAUDE.md:
- the AI judges and Kai reasons;
- code checks around them;
- the judge stays generous about wording, not about the key.

Typical places:
- a fact's `key`, `assume` or `hunch` in `topics/bandits/graph.draft.json` (then `pnpm content bundle bandits && pnpm content validate bandits`);
- the judge's prompt in `engine/tutor/judge.ts`;
- the reaction prompt in `engine/tutor/writer.ts`;
- a question in `topics/bandits/script.json`.

After a change:
1. Rerun the eval that failed, and `pnpm eval:judge` after any judge change.
2. Run `pnpm test && pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm build && pnpm e2e`.
3. Note what changed and why in CLAUDE.md (dated), and commit one change at a time.

Pushing deploys the live site that the pilot runs on, so push only when your teammate agrees, and not during a pilot session. If a fix would change what the study measures (the facts, the questions, the test items, the times), ask Phil's team first.
