## What changed and why

The team worried that three practice problems were too few for the conditions to make a difference. So practice now has a fourth problem and 17 minutes instead of 12, and the final test swapped one item so it doesn't contain a twin of the new problem.

**P8, "Radio playlists"** (`topics/bandits/practice.json`, last of four: P7, P3, P2, P8):

> A radio app picks one of four playlists for each hour with UCB, and scores each hour between 0 and 1 by how many listeners stay tuned. One playlist, Jazz, is clearly the least liked, and by hour 1,000 the app's averages show this. Around hour 1,000, UCB played Jazz about once every 100 hours. The app keeps running for years. How often does UCB play Jazz around hour 50,000?
>
> A. About once every 100 hours, as around hour 1,000
> B. It no longer plays Jazz at all
> C. **Far less often than once every 100 hours** (key)
> D. More often than once every 100 hours

- **Needs** (the gate, in step order): `n11.f1` (UCB's score, the highest wins), `n12.f1` (a pick shrinks its own bonus) and `n12.f2` (an unpicked bonus keeps growing, **ever more slowly**).
- **P8 is the first problem that needs the slowing.** P2 and P3 accept "the bonus grows" without it, because their vague reading still leads to the key. Kai's questions never ask for the slowing, so it is in the notebook only when a learner says it unprompted. Expect Kai to fail P8 for most learners, with the steady-pace guess (A). That is intended: it is a fair, named cause, and it is the idea test item V21 asks about.
- **Kai's guesses** (each fact's `assume`, word for word; `pnpm content validate` checks them):

  | The notebook | Kai's guess (hunch) | Answer |
  |---|---|---|
  | nothing right on UCB's rule | "I'd guess UCB just goes for the option it knows least about, the one tried the fewest times." Jazz is the least tried, so it gets played more | D |
  | every bonus shrinks, or nothing on picked bonuses | "I'd guess every bonus just shrinks as the rounds go by, picked or not." | B |
  | nothing on unpicked bonuses | "I'd guess a bonus just stays put while its option isn't picked." | B |
  | unpicked bonuses grow, without the slowing | "The way I picture it, the bonus grows at a steady pace, so a neglected option comes back every so often." | A |
  | all three, correct | (its own steps) | C |

- **Why it's built this way:**
  - It is concrete (one observed gap, and a question about a much later one) rather than "how does UCB treat Jazz from then on?". A teammate pointed out that a general question makes the correction the rule itself. Here Kai makes a specific wrong prediction, and the learner has to find the rule behind it.
  - It is about UCB only: greedy is practised in P7 and P3.
  - It is the near twin of V4, so V4 left the final test (below).
- **The numbers are the lesson's formula simulated:** average + √(ln t / n), four playlists averaging 0.7, 0.6, 0.5 and 0.3, scores with sd 0.25 kept between 0 and 1, 40 runs.
  - Around hour 1,000 Jazz came back every 89 hours on average (single runs 39 to 153).
  - Around hour 50,000 it came back every 4,700 hours (2,100 to 9,100).
  - Every run still played it after hour 40,000.
  - `tests/practice.test.ts` recomputes these with a seeded simulation (`tests/bandit-sim.ts`).
- **No caveat on P8.** The caveat rule is unchanged (`caveatFor`): P3's and P2's caveats come into play only when Kai would solve every problem after them, which now includes P8. So they come into play only when the slowing was taught; otherwise Kai fails P8 from the gap instead. P7's caveats are `alone` and unaffected.

## When something fails

- **`without n11.f1` under 8/10 at D** (the model answers A, "rotating, so as before"): read the attempts.
  - If the model reasons consistently to A, change that belief's `answer` in practice.json from 3 to 0.
  - Then update `tests/practice.test.ts`: the `"D"` expectations for "no UCB rule" and the empty notebook become `"A"`, and D is then only the learner's belief "more plays".
  - Don't change the hunch: it is shared with every problem.
- **`full` under 9/10:** read what the model did. Don't loosen the gate.
  - **Model picks B ("levelling off"):** the eval's note for n12.f2 says "slower and slower, and it never stops", so this would be a solver slip.
  - **Model picks A:** the model ignored the slowing note.
  - Either way, try one wording change to the stem first, e.g. "by hour 1,000 …" → "after the first month …". Keep the 1,000 and 50,000 hours, which the test recomputes.
- **`vague n12.f2` under 8/10 at A:** the model probably used what it knows (that the growth slows) and answered C. The code replaces a right answer without the facts, so learners are safe, but it costs retries and time. Check `--audit`; if it's frequent, report it.
- **Leaks flagged in more than about 1 of 10 attempts:** report them with the wording. The solver prompt is shared with P7, P3 and P2, so only change it if you rerun all four.
- **If P8 can't be made to work today:** the fallback is the stashed P4 (audio tours, UCB's pick in one round; checked before), placed second, with V4 back in the final test in V21's place. That's a team decision: ask before doing it.
