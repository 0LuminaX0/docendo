## What changed and why

The team wanted more weight on greedy being able to change its favourite, without changing the facts (that would have invalidated the pilots). So P7's planted mistake (its `caveat`) changed in commit `96b3d93`, merged with Andrii's `4c6e5f5` (the judge without word lists) and `c3ff8b2` (follow-ups back) in `74ce7c2`.

**Before:** P7 had one planted mistake (A's one-day 7.5 comes down the way B's lucky 9 did, so greedy goes back to B). Like every planted mistake, it came into play only when Kai would solve all three problems and hadn't been wrong yet, so most learners never saw it.

**Now:** P7 has two planted mistakes, tried in order. They come into play whenever Kai could solve P7 (its gate open: greedy's rule taught), whatever happens on P3 and P2 (`alone: true`):

1. **`sticks`**: Kai's notes on greedy and lock-in (ideas n05, n06) don't say greedy can change its favourite. Kai reads them as "B won the trials, so greedy sticks with its favourite."
   - What counts as saying it is the `covered` pattern: switch, overtake, a new leader or favourite, the favourite changes, every round/day/time, anew, so far, currently, right now, at that moment, update or recalculate, falls/drops below, goes (back) to the other/better one, no longer the best.
   - "Greedy picks the best option it knows" (R's note) and "greedy always goes for the highest average" leave it open.
2. **`lockin`**: the notes say greedy switches, Kai has a note on lock-in (`requires: ["n06.f1"]`, partial counts), and the notes on averages, greedy and lock-in (n03, n05, n06, n16) don't say what tells lock-in from switching.
   - The missing point: a featured option's average moves towards its own true average, so lock-in needs an average stuck below the others' true averages.
   - Kai holds two rules that pull different ways and applies lock-in to A ("A started worse than B").

Both forms answer option A ("B on day 5 and after"), which is also what greedy's wrong belief gives when greedy's rule wasn't taught. Notes that say both points: Kai solves P7, and P3's or P2's planted mistake can come later by the old rule.

**How it works** (`engine/tutor/solve.ts`, `content/practice.ts`, `topics/bandits/practice.json`):
- `caveatFor` decides, before Kai's first try at a problem, whether a planted mistake is in play. `caveatsOf(item)` gives a problem's list: one object, or an array tried in order.
- Each entry has:
  - `id`, logged as `caveatId` on `practice_kai_solve`;
  - `about`, the correct point, shown to the solver;
  - `misreading`, Kai's wrong reading, and `answer`, its option;
  - `covered`, the pattern;
  - `ideas`, the ideas whose notes the pattern reads (without it, every note);
  - `requires` and `alone`;
  - `opening`, the offline attempt's first step.
- When one is in play, the solver gets the extra `CAVEAT` paragraph: follow the notes if they say the point in any words, otherwise read them this way, never against a note. Code accepts exactly two answers:
  - the planted one, logged `misread`;
  - the key, logged `covered`: the model judged the notes say it.

  Anything else, or a turnaround, gets a stricter retry, then `offlineSolve`.

## When something fails

- **`caveat lockin` under 8 of 10**, the expected weak spot. With a switching note and B's 7 under A's 7.5 in front of it, the model may judge the notes settle it and answer right. That's accepted and logged `covered`, so the mistake simply doesn't appear.
  1. Sharpen `lockin`'s `about` or `misreading` in `practice.json`, keeping it Kai's reading of its own notes, never against one. For example, make the misreading cite the lock-in note more plainly.
  2. If it still doesn't land, delete the `lockin` entry: `sticks` stands on its own.
- **`said` cases under 9 of 10** (Kai goes against a note): don't loosen anything. Fix the wording of that entry, or narrow its `covered` pattern's reach. Leave the shared `CAVEAT` prompt alone unless P3 and P2 are rechecked too; their numbers depend on it.
- **`caveat sticks` under 8 of 10:** read the attempts. Usually the model read "the best option" as recomputed every day. Sharpen the misreading.
- **The pattern calls notes "covered" that don't say greedy switches**, or the reverse: adjust `covered` in `practice.json`, and add the wording to the lists in the test "P7: tell notes that say greedy can change its favourite from notes that don't" in `tests/practice.test.ts`.

After a change: rerun the failing eval, `pnpm test && pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm content validate bandits`, `pnpm build && pnpm e2e`. Note it in CLAUDE.md (dated), commit, and push only between sessions.
