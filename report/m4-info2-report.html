Can you take part? Yes, if all of these hold:

  • you are 18 or older and study computer science or another engineering field at a university;
  • you have taken a course on probability or statistics;
  • you have not studied multi-armed bandits (or algorithms for them, such as UCB), and have no formal training in reinforcement learning (a rough idea of it is fine).

How to start

Scan the code to join our study group. You get your personal access code and all the details there.

Plan about an hour in one go somewhere quiet. If you can, sit where you can talk out loud, with a microphone: speaking to the AI is easier than typing.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary and unpaid, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason. You can have your data deleted until 6 December 2026: send us your access code.

What we record

Your answers, your messages to the AI (as text, never audio) and how you use the site, under a random session number: never your name, email or IP address. AI models process your messages through OpenRouter, only with providers that do not store them.
